# LoL Warm-Up

A short set of browser drills to warm up your League of Legends mechanics before you queue.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a desktop browser. Nothing to install or build.

Use **Start full routine** (about 3 minutes) to run every drill back to back, or pick a single drill. Personal bests are saved in your browser.

## Drills

| Drill | Length | What it trains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target Clicks | 30s | Snapping to and clicking targets quickly and accurately |
| Key Combos | 30s | Fast, accurate presses of Q W E R D F |
| Last Hitting | 60s | Timing autos (windup plus missile travel) to land killing blows |
| Skillshot Dodging | 45s | Click-to-move dodging of telegraphed skillshots, plus Flash (F or D) |

Drills use right-click to move and attack, like the game. If you play with left-click, tick **I move and attack with left-click** on the menu.

Press `Esc` during a drill to stop it.

## Suggested in-client follow-up

After the routine, if you have time, spend 5 minutes in Practice Tool on your main champion: last-hit one wave with no abilities, then run through your main combos on the target dummy.
